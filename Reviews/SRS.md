# Spaced Repetition System

Base interval: 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days → 30 days

## Mistake-Based Priority

- Add every corrected mistake to `Data/srs_queue.json`.
- Increase priority when the mistake matches a known weak point.
- Review due items before teaching new grammar.
- If reviews are due, do not introduce new items in the same turn.
- Retest immediately with a similar sentence after correction.

## Current High-Priority Targets

1. が vs を
2. たいです
3. から vs ので
