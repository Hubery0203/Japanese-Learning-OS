# Dataset attribution

`vocabulary.json` and `kanji.json` incorporate the N5 CSV datasets from [OpenJLPT](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), by Evan Clan, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

- Source files: `data/csv/vocab-n5.csv` and `data/csv/kanji-n5.csv`.
- Retrieved 2026-10-24.
- Adaptations: stable repository IDs were assigned; source vocabulary entries were combined with the repository's existing curated entries; the old Chinese glosses were retained where available; source English glosses and parts of speech are retained; source example sentences were omitted.
- The resulting adapted datasets are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. They are candidate study lists, not official JLPT specifications. JLPT states it does not publish fixed vocabulary/kanji test-content lists.
